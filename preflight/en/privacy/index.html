Preflight Privacy Policy

Preflight performs supported scanning and redaction on the device. The app does not require an account and does not upload the documents you process. Files are exported only when you choose an action. Purchase validation is handled by Apple. You can delete local history and temporary files from Settings.

To provide one free verified export while preventing the allowance from resetting after reinstall, Preflight verifies an App Store transaction identity supplied by Apple, hashes it on the device, and checks an anonymous allowance record in iCloud. CloudKit stores only the hashed record name and the fact that the free export was used. It does not receive the original identity, documents, images or extracted text. The free allowance check requires an iCloud account and internet connection. Monthly, annual and lifetime purchase access is validated by Apple.

When you choose AI Safe Text, text extraction and OCR for images and PDFs also run entirely on the device. The anonymized text does not preserve the source document’s layout. Imported copies and processing files may remain temporarily in a protected local working directory until the app’s cleanup process removes them. Extracted text is not uploaded.
